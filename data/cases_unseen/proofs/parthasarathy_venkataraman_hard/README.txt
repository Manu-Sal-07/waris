Proofs for the family of the late Parthasarathy Venkataraman   (hard)

1. In the Waris app, choose 'Browse files', open this folder, select ALL the images in it (Ctrl+A) and upload them.
2. Fill the form:
     Claim for ........ Bank deposits, Shares
     I am claiming as . Legal heir
     Other names ...... (leave empty)
3. Check 'What Waris read', fix anything it got wrong, then press 'Start'.

Later: the family finds more papers (folder 'later'). After the first result, upload those files with 'Add more documents' to see the case continue.
