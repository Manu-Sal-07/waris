Death claim on a life insurance policy where a nominee is named. The nominee submits the insurer's claim form,
the death certificate, the original policy bond and KYC of the nominee.
NOTE (deliberate corpus gap): there is no rule for the case where the nominee has also died. Waris must say
"I don't know" for that situation instead of improvising.
