Transmission of shares of a deceased holder to the nominee registered with the company. The nominee submits the
transmission request form, the death certificate and own KYC to the registrar. Unpaid dividends follow the same
transmission. This rule covers a nominee claimant only; joint holders of shares are not covered in this corpus.
