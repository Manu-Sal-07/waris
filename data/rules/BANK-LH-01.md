Legal heir procedure for a deposit up to five lakh rupees (illustrative threshold) when there is no nominee on the account. The legal heir must submit the bank's claim form,
the death certificate, a legal heir certificate (or succession certificate), an indemnity bond, and KYC of the
claimant. The bank pays the heirs after verifying heirship documents.
