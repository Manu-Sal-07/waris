Claim of a deceased account holder's deposit by the registered nominee. Documents required to claim the deposit:
the bank's claim form, the death certificate of the deceased account holder, and KYC of the nominee.
The bank settles the deposit claim in favour of the nominee named on the account. Simplified deposit claim
procedure for a bank deposit where a nominee is registered.
