Death claim on a life insurance policy that has no nominee on record. The legal heirs submit the insurer's claim
form, the death certificate, the original policy bond, a succession certificate and KYC of the claimant.
This rule covers only policies with no nominee named. It does not cover the case where a nominee was named
and has also died: that situation is deliberately left out of the corpus.
