title: Proof required when the depositor's name differs between bank record and death certificate
institution_type: bank
topic: name_variation
applies_to: any
amount_band: any

---
Name variation between the bank record and the death certificate (initials, spelling, name order). The claimant must
provide an affidavit explaining that the names in the records refer to the same person, before the bank will process
the claim.
