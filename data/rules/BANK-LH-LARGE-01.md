Legal heir procedure for a large deposit (above five lakh rupees) when there is no nominee. Banks usually ask for a
court-issued succession certificate instead of a legal heir certificate, together with the claim form, the death
certificate, an indemnity bond, KYC of the claimant and no-objection statements from the other legal heirs.
The amount threshold is illustrative; each bank sets its own limit.
