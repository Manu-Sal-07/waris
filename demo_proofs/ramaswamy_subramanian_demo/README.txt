Proofs for the family of the late Ramaswamy Subramanian   (the classic demo case)

1. In the Waris app, choose 'Browse files', open this folder, select ALL the images in it (Ctrl+A) and upload them.
2. Fill the form:
     Claim for ........ Bank deposits, Life insurance, Shares
     I am claiming as . Legal heir
     Other names ...... (leave empty)
3. Check 'What Waris read', then press 'Start'.
